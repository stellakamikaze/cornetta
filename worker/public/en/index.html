Your voice is as loud as theirs

Install it in two minutesthree steps.

You do it once. Updates arrive on their own.

  1. Download and drag

    Download Cornetta, open the file from your Downloads folder and drag the icon into Applications.

  2. Allow the first launch

    macOS blocks free apps that do not come from the App Store. Click Done, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

  3. Grant two permissions

    On the first recording Cornetta asks for the Microphone, for your voice, and System Audio Recording, for the others. Click Allow for both.

If you open Cornetta straight from the disk image, it offers to move itself to Applications: accept. From there it updates itself and macOS remembers the permissions.

  1. Download and open

    Download Cornetta and open the CornettaSetup.exe file. No administrator rights needed.

  2. Allow the first launch

    Windows may show the warning Windows protected your PC, because the installer is not signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.

    Cornetta appears in the notification area, next to the clock. If you do not see it, open the ^ arrow beside it.

If the microphone stays silent, open Settings › Privacy & security › Microphone and turn on microphone access for desktop apps.

Questions

Is Cornetta free?

Yes. Free, with no account, no subscription and no ads.

Which apps does it work with?

Any app that uses the Mac's microphone and audio: WhatsApp, FaceTime, Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord, Telegram, Skype and calls in the browser.

Is it legal to record a call?

It depends on where you live. In Italy and other one-party-consent places you may record a conversation you take part in for personal use; sharing it without consent is not allowed.

Other countries and some US states require everyone's consent. Check your local law before recording.

Do I need BlackHole or another audio driver?

No. Cornetta uses the macOS feature that records system audio (Core Audio process taps, macOS 14.2 and later). It installs no driver and does not change your audio settings.

Does Cornetta transcribe calls?

Yes, on macOS 26 or later. When you stop recording, Cornetta transcribes the call on your Mac with Apple speech recognition, separates your voice from the others and also saves the text as an .md file next to the audio. Nothing is sent to the internet.

Find it in the Recordings window, from the menu bar icon or with ⌘ T.

Where are the recordings saved?

In Music › Cornetta, as .m4a files (AAC, 48 kHz, stereo) named with date and time. They stay on your Mac: Cornetta never uploads them.

Does it work with AirPods?

Yes. During calls, though, macOS switches to the Bluetooth voice profile, which sounds worse: that applies to every recorder. If you can, use the Mac's microphone.

Does it work on iPhone or Android?

No. iOS gives no app access to call audio, and Android blocks it for regular apps since Android 10. Cornetta runs on your computer.

What about Windows?

The Windows version is in development. This page will offer it as soon as it is ready.

Yes, in beta: download Cornetta for Windows. It runs on Windows 10 and 11 and installs without administrator rights. On first launch Windows may show a warning: click More info and then Run anyway.

It records and detects calls like the Mac app. Transcription is Mac-only for now.

Is Cornetta open source?

Yes. The code for the Mac and Windows apps is public on GitHub under the MIT license. You can read it, build it yourself and suggest changes.

Does Cornetta collect data?

Recordings and transcripts never leave your computer.

The website counts visits without cookies, with a service on the developer's own server. The app sends anonymous usage data: version, system, recording outcomes and error codes. Never audio, text, file names or contacts. Turn it off from the app menu with Invia dati di funzionamento (Send usage data).

Why does macOS say it cannot verify the developer?

Cornetta is free and not distributed through the App Store, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.
